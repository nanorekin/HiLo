How to play

Goal

You're shown a coin, a date and its price. Below it is another coin and date with the price hidden. Guess whether that price was higher or lower than the one above. Guess right and the hidden card becomes the new current card, and a fresh card appears below it. Keep the chain going as long as you can.

The data

All prices are rounded, publicly reported historical figures, checked against news and market-data sources. They are approximations for a game, not exact exchange data or financial advice, and nothing here is a prediction about future prices.

Scoring

Each correct guess is worth more the longer your current streak is. A wrong guess resets the streak.

Modes

Daily: the same 15-card chain for everyone, new at 00:00 UTC. Reach the end without a wrong guess to clear the day. Quick 60: 60 seconds of guesses — a wrong one just resets your streak and the game carries on. Survival: a classic chain that ends on your first wrong guess; see how long you can go.

Skip

Not sure about the next card? Skip it once per round for a fresh one, free of charge to your streak. Supporters get two skips.

Keyboard

Press the up arrow or H for Higher, the down arrow or L for Lower, and Enter or Space for Next.

Ranks

Total correct guesses earn a rank: Newbie, Hodler (50), Diamond Hands (150), Whale (400) and Satoshi (1,000).

Supporters

A tip of 0.01 XNO or more unlocks the extra accent colours and a second skip. Tips are optional and the whole game is free.
